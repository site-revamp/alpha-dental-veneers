Where are Alpha Dental Care practices located?

Alpha Dental Care lists practices across England, including locations in County Durham, Manchester, the Midlands, North East and Northumberland, South East and Essex, Teesside and North Yorkshire, and South Yorkshire. Visit the practice directory for the current list.

Does Alpha Dental Care offer both NHS and private dentistry?

Yes. Alpha Dental Care offers NHS and private dental treatments. The services and availability can vary by practice, so contact your local team for details.

What is Dental Essentials?

Dental Essentials is an independent, pay-as-you-go dental scheme offered by Alpha Dental Care. The published scheme starts from £39 and has no contract or monthly costs. Review the official scheme page for current options, inclusions, fees and terms.

How can I find out whether a practice is accepting NHS patients?

NHS availability can vary. Check the NHS dentistry information on the Alpha Dental Care website and contact your nearest practice for current waiting-list information.

What should I do if I need emergency dental care?

Contact your local Alpha Dental Care practice as soon as possible. The team can offer advice and arrange an emergency appointment where available. Visit the emergency dentistry page for further guidance.

How do I arrange an appointment?

Start by finding your nearest practice through the official location directory. Each practice's listing provides the relevant contact and appointment information.
