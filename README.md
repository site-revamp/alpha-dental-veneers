Alpha Dental Care Website Redesign
1. Website Overview
Alpha Dental Care is a UK-based multi-location dental practice group providing NHS, private and specialist dental treatments across several regions of England.

The group offers general, cosmetic and restorative dentistry, alongside specialist services such as periodontal therapy, conscious sedation and dental hygiene.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Alpha Dental Care as a connected network of dental practices. It targets individuals and families seeking accessible dental care, cosmetic treatments and specialist dental services.

The design direction prioritises clear service communication, easier practice discovery, consistent group branding and a straightforward patient journey.
2. Main Improvements
1. Improved Dental Service Organisation
- Reorganised the presentation of general, cosmetic, restorative and specialist dental treatments.
- Improved the distinction between services such as check-ups, fillings, Invisalign, composite bonding, implants and dentures.
- Gave greater clarity to the group's broad treatment offering.
- Addresses the challenge of helping visitors navigate a diverse range of dental services across the practice network.
2. Stronger Multi-Location Practice Presentation
- Improved the organisation and visibility of the group's practice locations.
- Strengthened the connection between the central brand and its individual dental practices.
- Made regional practice discovery a clearer part of the homepage structure.
- Addresses the difficulty of understanding the group's geographic coverage and finding a relevant local practice.
3. Clearer Patient Journey and Service Accessibility
- Refined the homepage content flow to help visitors understand available treatments and identify relevant practice information.
- Improved the presentation of NHS, private and specialist care offerings.
- Incorporated Dental Essentials as a supporting part of the group's dental care proposition.
- Addresses the need for clearer communication around treatment options and the different ways patients can access dental care.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces a more structured and consistent presentation for a multi-location dental group.

Key improvements include:
- Visual presentation: A more cohesive group-level appearance that supports the healthcare and dental care positioning.
- Layout: Better organisation of treatment categories, practice information and supporting services.
- Typography: Clearer content hierarchy and improved readability.
- Consistency: A more unified presentation of the group's services and multi-location identity.
- User experience: A more logical browsing journey from exploring treatments to discovering local practices.
- Content organisation: Improved separation of general, cosmetic, restorative and specialist dental services.
